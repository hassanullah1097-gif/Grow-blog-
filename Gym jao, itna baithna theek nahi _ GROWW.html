Baithna kyun masla hai

Jab hum lambay waqt tak baithe rehte hain to muscles kam kaam karte hain, calories kam jalti hain aur kamar, gardan aur kandhon par bojh barhta hai. Sehat ke idaray bhi baar baar kehte hain ke zyada baithna sehat ke liye achha nahi, aur rozana harkat zaroori hai.

Dua ye nahi ke aap athlete banein. Maqsad ye hai ke jism ko roz thora sa kaam mile.

Aam tor par balighon ke liye hafte mein kam az kam 150 minute halki se darmiyani exercise ka mashwara diya jata hai. Yani agar aap gym nahi bhi ja rahe, tab bhi chalna aur harkat is hisse ko poora karne mein madad deti hai.

Gym kaise shuru karein

  • Chhota shuru karein. Pehle hafte mein sirf 2 se 3 din, 30 se 40 minute kafi hain.
  • Warm-up zaroor karein. 5 se 10 minute walking ya halki stretching, phir weights.
  • Pehle form, phir weight. Halka weight lekar sahi posture seekhein. Zakhmi hone se behtar hai ke dheere chalein.
  • Poore jism ko kaam dein. Taangein, seena, peeth, kandhe aur pet, sab ke liye kuch na kuch ho.
  • Pani, neend aur khana. Pani peeyen, 7 se 8 ghante soyein aur protein wali cheezein khayein. Neend ke baghair recovery nahi hoti.

Desk par rehte hue

  • Har 30 se 60 minute baad khare ho kar 2 minute chalein.
  • Phone par baat karte waqt khare ho jayen.
  • Lift ki jagah seedhiyan istemal karein.
  • Kursi par seedha baithein, kamar ko sahara dein aur screen aankhon ke barabar rakhein.

Aam ghaltiyan

  • Pehle din hi bohot zyada weight utha lena.
  • Warm-up aur stretching chhor dena.
  • Roz gym jana aur jism ko aaram na dena.
  • Dosron se muqabla karna. Apna muqabla sirf kal ke apne aap se karein.

Note: Yeh aam maloomat hai, doctor ka mashwara nahi. Agar aapko dil, jorhon, blood pressure ya kisi bimari ka masla hai ya dard mehsoos ho, to shuru karne se pehle doctor se zaroor poochein.
